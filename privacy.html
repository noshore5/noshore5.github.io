Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CoherIQs Inc. ("CoherIQs", "we") handles information in connection with this website and the CoherIQs API (the "Service").

Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, email address, and organization, when you contact us or request beta access.
  • API request data submitted to the Service, which may include windows of multi-channel EEG signal data and associated parameters (sampling rate, channel labels).
  • Technical logs generated automatically, such as IP address, timestamps, request metadata, and error information.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and secure the Service.
  • To respond to inquiries and manage beta access.
  • To debug, evaluate, and improve the Service and its models.

Biomedical signal data

EEG data submitted to the API is processed to return a score and is not linked to an identifiable individual unless the submitting party includes identifiers in the request. Callers are responsible for obtaining any consent required to submit such data and for not transmitting direct identifiers unless necessary.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers who process it on our behalf (for example, cloud hosting), or where required by law.

Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business or legal purposes. You may request deletion of information associated with you or your account by contacting us.
