EEG seizure prediction

How the model turns 30 seconds of EEG into a seizure-risk score

Every pair of electrodes gets a coherence score describing how in-sync their brain activity is. That network of scores is fed through a sequence model (Mamba) that tracks how the network evolves over the 30-second window, then collapsed to a single prediction. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and hover any box for more detail.

At a glance: 23 EEG electrodes  →  253 electrode-pair connections  ·  each connection tracked as a 2-part (real / imaginary) coherence signal across 8 frequency bands  ·  a shared sequence model reads how the whole network changes over the 30-second window  ·  output: seizure-risk score.
